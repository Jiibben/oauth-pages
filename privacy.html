Privacy policy

Jibben Drive Sync is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own devices, with the owner's own Google account.

  • Google user data (Drive files) is only copied between the owner's devices and the owner's Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, stored on third-party servers, shared, sold or used for any other purpose.
  • OAuth tokens stay on the owner's devices. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
